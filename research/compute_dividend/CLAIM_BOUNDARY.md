# Claim boundary

## Supported, bounded

- On exact natural-number inputs satisfying the stated assumptions, the Lean
  theorems establish rent conservation, the gross-rent floor threshold,
  rejection no-op, accepted-reserve conservation, uniform-floor feasibility,
  a generic discrete-concave one-atom transfer inequality, the arithmetic fact
  that a true bounded loss below wealth-minus-floor preserves that floor, and
  Boolean gate implications.
- The checked Python greedy allocator equals an independently enumerated optimum
  in 10,836 named finite cases.
- The atom-by-atom reference allocator rejects budgets above 10,000 atoms; a
  scalable production allocator and unrestricted optimality proof remain open.
- Both checked-in Tau packets contain all 256 Boolean rows, have no duplicates,
  accept only the all-true row, and match the Python/Lean conjunctions
  semantically.
- A clean source-pinned Tau candidate with SHA-256 `b2699306...` natively
  produced canonical output for V1.1 and both new packets.  Its binary and
  exact version identity differ from the reviewed replay identity, so this is
  candidate-only execution evidence.
- The reference wealth selector cannot select the tested high-score leveraged
  plan and returns a no-op when no admitted plan beats the no-op score.

## Under test

- Whether a U.S. data-center contract can generate a stable net public rent
  after full incremental cost recovery.
- Whether cash and compute benefits raise pre-registered concave household
  welfare without worsening ratepayer, reliability, or environmental outcomes.
- Whether the eligibility system resists duplicate identities and exclusion.
- Whether a legally compliant household agent improves after-fee, risk-adjusted
  outcomes relative to simple baselines.
- Interpreter execution of the new packets and the V1.1 packet on the exact
  reviewed Tau binary.

## Explicitly not claimed

- Source commit `fd137e8` or parser pin `5dd036...` produced the reviewed Linux
  binary; the replay runner only verifies binary bytes, reported version,
  packet, and output.
- The host environment, kernel, dynamic libraries, hardware, or build image is
  attested or hermetic.
- Tau or Tau Net authenticates external facts, identity, compute delivery,
  policy legitimacy, custody, settlement, or legal compliance.
- Any asset return, FIRE date, Buffett-level skill, capital preservation,
  suitability, fiduciary compliance, or generational wealth.
- The finite scenario envelope controls out-of-sample loss.
- The modeled empirical tail-loss statistic supplies the true loss premise of
  the protected-floor theorem.
- Concavity remains poor-first under arbitrary household priority weights.
- U.S. data-center deployment is socially beneficial, environmentally
  sustainable, or preferable to alternatives.
- A formal proof of a policy-relative gate is a proof of objective ethics or
  global sociotechnical alignment.
