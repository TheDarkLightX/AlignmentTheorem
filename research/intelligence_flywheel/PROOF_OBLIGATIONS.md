# Proof and evidence obligations

## PO-IF-01 — causal bridge identification

Estimate a preregistered chain from task-level capability to productivity, unit
cost, quality-adjusted essential-basket price, pass-through, household access,
and externality-adjusted price.  Report uncertainty and failed links.  A model
fit is not a causal result without an identification strategy.  Preferred next
lane: SAGE or an equivalent econometric/causal research adapter, currently
unavailable.

## PO-IF-02 — authenticated receipt refinement

Define canonical signed/committed receipts for policy root, capability tests,
productivity bridge, basket gain, funded floor, concentration, grid/externality,
debt, nonce/epoch, and strict margin.  Prove that the host/oracle verifier maps
accepted receipts to exactly the nine Tau facts.  Preferred lanes: ESSO/model
checking plus Lean refinement.  Current custom streams do not satisfy this.

## PO-IF-03 — Tau Net end-to-end enforcement

At a pinned Testnet revision, deploy the sender-scoped rule through the normal
application-rule path and test admission, block apply, state hash, mutation,
missing evidence, replay/stale epoch, reorg, governance update, and multi-node
determinism.  The direct native ABI result is completed evidence for syntax and
predicate evaluation only.

## PO-IF-04 — reviewed Tau binary replay

Supply the exact Linux executable with SHA-256 `c4926740...`, let the strict
runner snapshot and preflight it, verify the exact version, execute the closed
512-row packet, and require byte-identical output.  Do not infer source-to-
binary provenance or environment attestation from a successful replay.

## PO-IF-05 — unrestricted growth-rate theorem

Extend the existing witness theorem to a carefully quantified eventual-
dominance theorem over an appropriate ordered field or asymptotic structure,
including all positivity premises and strictness.  Keep the empirical bridge
outside the formal conclusion.  The current Lean theorem deliberately accepts
a crossing witness and does not claim to prove the economic premises.

## PO-IF-06 — distributional and debt safety

Test the universal floor and prioritarian welfare objective against benefit
capture, identity exclusion, regional/grid burden, labor displacement,
essential-service quality, nominal debt, and agent losses.  Pre-register
distributional outcomes and stopping rules.  Passing a Boolean gate is not an
empirical welfare result.
