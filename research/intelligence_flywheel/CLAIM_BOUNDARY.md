# Claim boundary after this work

## Supported, bounded

- The exact bridge equation implies a positive price floor whenever automation
  or pass-through is incomplete; the finite exact model checked 544 named
  parameter/epoch cases with no identity or floor violations.
- Under explicit geometric deviation/reward bounds and a supplied strict
  crossing witness, the Lean theorem proves `B_n < M_n*K_n`.
- Lean proves a constructive fixed-deviation doubling witness, a generic
  bounded-reward impossibility theorem, strict rejection at equality, the
  nine-fact gate implication, and non-treasury sender isolation.
- The complete 512-row Tau packet, Python predicate, and Lean predicate are the
  same nine-fact conjunction and accept only the all-true assignment.
- The source-pinned local Tau candidate produced the exact 512-row output.  It
  is candidate-only evidence and is not promotion-eligible.
- At pinned Tau Testnet and Tau source revisions, the direct native `o5` ABI
  executed the sender-scoped rule and matched all 12 positive/mutation/scope/
  missing-input cases.

## Under test or pending

- Whether any operational capability measure follows a stable doubling law.
- Whether capability gains causally reduce an essential household basket at
  the assumed elasticity and pass-through.
- Whether a DAC earns a persistent positive net return after energy, compute,
  capital, reliability, environmental, and governance costs.
- Whether a lawful data-center rent funds a universal benefit and household
  compute/wealth copilot without cost shifting, exclusion, or concentration.
- Full node admission/block-apply replay, governance activation, multi-node
  determinism, reorg behavior, and economic finality for the Tau Testnet rule.
- Replay on the reviewed `c4926740...` Tau Linux binary.
- A consensus-recognized oracle/receipt scheme authenticating `i17..i25`.

## Explicitly not claimed

- Intelligence doubling is an observed general law or is sufficient for
  hyperdeflation, alignment, or abundance.
- Hyperdeflation applies to the entire CPI, is always desirable, or is safe for
  households with nominal debts.
- The assumptions `a`, `rho`, `eta`, DAC return, benefit capture, or deviation
  growth have been empirically estimated.
- Current source commit `9b191af...` produced the local candidate or native
  module, and historical source commit `fd137e8...` produced the reviewed
  binary; none of those source-to-binary relationships is independently attested.
- The kernel, dynamic libraries, hardware, build image, or execution environment
  is hermetically attested.
- The direct ABI probe proves node deployment or public-network deployment, consensus finality,
  production readiness, or safety for real funds.
- Tau authenticates capability, productivity, prices, welfare, identity, grid
  externalities, debt state, legal authority, custody, or receipts by itself.
- Submitters supplying true custom-input bits authenticate the corresponding
  economic facts; the current ABI needs a separate trusted receipt verifier.
- A personal agent guarantees Buffett-level skill, returns, FIRE, generational
  wealth, capital preservation, suitability, or fiduciary compliance.
- A formal policy-relative predicate proves objective ethics or global
  sociotechnical alignment.
- No non-Tau formal system could implement an analogous architecture.  The
  narrower claim is that current Tau Net policy arbitration is Tau-native.
