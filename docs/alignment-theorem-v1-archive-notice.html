Retained Version 1 result

Let M be the scarcity multiplier, R the funded direct-reward coefficient, L the scarcity-upside coefficient available only to an EETF-eligible branch, and G the bounded deviation gain available to the excluded branch. If exclusion is enforceable and G < M(R+L), every exact maximizer over the declared alternatives selects eligibility. For integers with R+L > 0, the least multiplier is floor(G/(R+L))+1. The standalone proof is in proofs/v1/AlignmentTheoremV1.lean.

Exclusion is opportunity cost

The historical presentation placed M L as a negative term on the excluded branch. Adding M L to both alternatives gives the equivalent normalized comparison M(R+L) > G. No tax, fine, burn, confiscation, or balance debit is applied to the excluded actor. The load-bearing premise is that the actor genuinely cannot obtain the protected scarcity upside. Upside shared by both branches cancels.
