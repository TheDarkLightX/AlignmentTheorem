The intelligence dividend

Data centers can be more than private infrastructure. Under demanding, testable assumptions, verified AI productivity could lower essential costs, fund a universal compute dividend, and power constrained personal wealth copilots. Tau can make the spending rule executable. It cannot make the economic facts true.

The decisive correction: “intelligence doubles” is not the Alignment Theorem. The theorem still requires protected real benefit to strictly exceed the benefit of deviation.

01 · theorem

Capability is upstream.
The margin is the claim.

Let I be verified task capability, P an accessible essential-basket price, M=1/P its purchasing-power multiplier, K protected benefit, and B deviation benefit.

\[P_t=1-a\rho+a\rho I_t^{-\eta},\quad M_t=P_t^{-1},\quad \operatorname{Aligned}(t)\iff B_t<M_tK_t\]
1 · Capabilitytask success, not vibes
2 · Productivitymeasured causal effect
3 · Unit costquality adjusted
4 · Price & accesscompetition + pass-through
5 · Protected rewardfloor + dividend + compute

The bridge parameters are not decorative. If either automatable share a or pass-through ρ is incomplete, the model has a floor δ=1−aρ>0, hence M<=1/δ. Capability may double forever while household purchasing power remains bounded.

Sufficient map

Full bridge + slower deviation

If I=2^t, a=ρ=η=1, and protected reward grows faster than deviation, a strict crossing follows.

Countermodel

Partial transmission

With a=3/4 and ρ=4/5, price stays above 2/5 and purchasing power below 5/2.

Countermodel

Rebound cancels savings

A growing grid/resource surcharge can exactly offset declining automated cost. Gross efficiency is not net abundance.

03 · populist mechanism

A public compute dividend,
not a blank-check subsidy.

The utilitarian-populist objective is prioritarian: maximize concave household welfare only after a universal floor and within concentration, grid, environmental, and debt guardrails.

Data-center social contract

  • Recover incremental grid and public costs first.
  • Fund a universal cash and compute floor.
  • Allocate surplus with diminishing marginal utility.
  • Cap household/operator concentration and permit unspent reserve.
  • Stop when reliability, environment, debt, or distributional guardrails fail.

The “personal Buffett” boundary

  • An untrusted model proposes plans; a deterministic policy filter admits them.
  • Fresh consent, authorized custody, no leverage/shorting, diversification, loss, fee, and turnover limits are required.
  • The safe default is abstention or a simple baseline.
  • No promise of returns, FIRE date, suitability, fiduciary compliance, or generational wealth.
\[\max_x\ \sum_h w_h\sum_{j=1}^{x_h}\frac{1}{c_h+j}\quad\text{s.t. universal floor, budget, and concentration caps}\]
04 · Tau Net

Why Tau here—and what
“only Tau” can honestly mean.

On the current Tau Net protocol path, the rule is necessarily a Tau specification because Tau is the native policy arbiter. The stronger universal exclusivity claim—that no other formal platform could implement a comparable design—is neither proved nor needed.

Native policy

Executable specification

The same decidable rule is the artifact reviewed by humans and evaluated for o5. The protocol commits rule text into state.

Revision

Governed policy change

Tau is designed for specifications that can reason about and revise specifications. That is unusually aligned with a living economic constitution.

Trust boundary

Facts stay external

Tau can reject a false Boolean. It cannot know that a price index, grid reserve, identity, signature, or welfare claim is true unless a trusted verifier binds it.
