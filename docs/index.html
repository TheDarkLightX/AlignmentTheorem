The Alignment Theorem, Version 1

The original paper proposes that increasing monetary scarcity can make ethical work uniquely profitable through ethical-score-gated rewards and exclusion from exclusive scarcity upside. The negative “penalty” term is the opportunity cost of that missed upside, never a tax, fine, burn, or balance debit. The reconstructed proof formalizes the exclusion comparison; V1.1 generalizes it.
