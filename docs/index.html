⚡ The Core Insight

📜 Explicit Policy

A versioned community policy defines acceptable observable actions. The theorem is policy-relative and does not claim objective moral truth.

🤖 Aligned Agents

LLM agents can follow ethical constraints while pursuing useful and profitable tasks. Any capable lab may train them against the public policy interface.

💰 Finite Incentives

Funded rewards and conservative enforcement must exceed bounded deviation gains, compliance costs, and optimizer error by a strict margin.

🛡️ Publication Gate

A deterministic Tau gate checks authenticated obligations. Rejection pays nothing and leaves the committed reserve unchanged.

∞ Version 1.1: Hyperdeflationary Alignment

Version 1.1 preserves the original scarcity-driven idea and repairs its zero-threshold proof. At each finite epoch, the purchasing-power multiplier must strictly dominate the complete deviation, compliance-cost, and optimizer-error bound.

M(t) × [ethical reward exposure + non-ethical forfeiture exposure]
> deviation gain + compliance cost + optimizer error

Exact Threshold

For coefficient K > 0 and opposing bound B, the least integer multiplier is floor(B/K) + 1.

Infinite-Scarcity Limit

An unbounded sequence of finite multipliers eventually crosses every fixed finite threshold.

AGI Relative Growth

Growing deviation opportunities are covered only when B(t)/K(t) eventually remains below M(t).

Explicit Counterexamples

Common scaling, faster deviation growth, zero exposure, forged EETF, and unfunded rewards remain failures.

📐 The Formal Proof

Step 1: Fix a versioned policy and an authenticated evidence boundary.
Step 2: Admit publication only when all seven policy obligations hold.
Step 3: Prove that every rejected settlement has zero payout and no reserve effect.
Step 4: Prove that every accepted payout is funded and conserves the reserve.
Step 5: Bound private deviation gain, compliance cost, and optimizer error.
Step 6: Require a strict finite incentive margin over those bounds.
Step 7: Conclude that every epsilon-optimal modeled choice is policy compliant.
∎ QED

The exact statement is machine checked in proofs/v2/AlignmentTheoremV2.lean. The conclusion remains conditional on the declared bounds and authenticated facts.

🤖 Why This Works for AI

✓ Feasible Joint Behavior

Present-day assistants show that an agent can pursue a task while following ethical and safety constraints. This establishes behavioral feasibility.

✓ Economic Reinforcement

When compliant work pays best, training and profit seeking point agents toward the same observable behavior.

✓ Verifier-Conditioned

The model proposes actions. Authenticated evidence and deterministic policy checks decide what the network can publish and reward.

△ Explicit Limits

The result does not prove universal generalization, objective ethics, honest sensors, coalition safety, or bounds outside the modeled action class.
